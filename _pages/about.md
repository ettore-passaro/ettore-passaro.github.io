I'm a software engineer with 10 years of experience across games, AR SDKs, mobile apps, and developer tooling, from co-founding a game studio to building software used by over a million developers worldwide.

Starting as a game developer at a studio I co-founded, I built games in Unity and plugins for its Editor, then moved on to PTC Inc. where I became the primary maintainer of the Unity Extension for the Vuforia Engine. There I led the development of the Vuforia Creator app for iOS, automated workflows and CI/CD pipelines, and maintained compatibility across many Mixed Reality platforms.

Currently building Aura, a migraine journal app, while deepening my expertise in SwiftUI, Kotlin, and modern cross-platform development.
