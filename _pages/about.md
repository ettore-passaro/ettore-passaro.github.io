I'm an Italian Senior Software Engineer based in Vienna, Austria.

I started my career in software engineering by co-founding a game studio in Italy years ago, inspired by the idea of a game I fell in love with and armed with basic Unity knowledge and plenty of Brackeys tutorials.  
Over the years, the scope of the company changed, and I worked on multiple different projects, ranging from mobile games to AR experiences. I kept expanding my skills with **Unity and C#**, building gameplay and tools for the Editor.

Eventually I moved on to join the team at PTC Inc. working on the Unity plugin for the Augmented Reality engine Vuforia. There I found that my passion for developer tools could go beyond some custom Editor plugins and extend to building the whole SDK plugin, its sample projects, build scripts in Python, and CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions.  
I also realised that the best way to improve the developers' experience is to actually communicate with them and understand their pain points, so I started answering questions on Stack Overflow and on a public GitHub repository where developers could report issues and suggest improvements.

Over the years at PTC, it became clear to me that programming was not the only thing I enjoyed about software engineering. I got involved in planning, team leadership, and product ownership, and I led the development of a new **native iOS app**, coordinating the effort between Product, UX and Engineering teams while being the main contributor to the architecture and the development of the app. By the time the app shipped to the App Store, I had spent more time writing **Swift and UIKit** than Unity, and realised I had quietly become an iOS developer alongside everything else.

After a layoff from PTC, I've been experimenting with many different technologies and projects I never had the time to explore in my previous roles, from Unreal Engine and Godot, to Android and embedded C programming.  
Eventually, I found my way back to mobile app development, and from a personal necessity, I started working on a migraine tracking app called [Aura](https://github.com/epassaro-dev/aura). Apart from the obvious goal of developing an app that could help me manage my migraines, I also wanted to learn more about modern app development on iOS and Android, from SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose to possibly Flutter at a later point. My objective is to keep sharpening and widening my mobile development skills, while also using this chance to get more familiar with AI-assisted development and Claude Code.

Mobile development is what I'm currently focused on. Games are where I started and where I'd love to return. But more than any specific domain, what I'm really looking forward to is the opportunity to work on something that can have a real positive impact on people. Whether through a game or a healthcare app, I'd rather work on something small that can make a difference than something big that might not.
